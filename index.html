ABOUT ME

My Computer Science Journey

Completing my Computer Science degree has given me the opportunity to develop my skills in programming, software engineering, databases, algorithms, and security.

Throughout the program, I learned that developing software is about more than simply making an application work. Good software should also be reliable, maintainable, secure, and designed with the user in mind.

My CS-499 capstone allowed me to apply these skills by returning to an Android Inventory Management System I created earlier in the program and improving it with new functionality, database improvements, algorithms, validation, testing, and security features.
